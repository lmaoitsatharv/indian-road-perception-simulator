# Picking a destination: decisions made while building it (8-9 November)

Every decision taken on the user's behalf while building [the design](CAMERA_DRIVING_DESTINATIONS_DESIGN_20261108.md) from [its plan](CAMERA_DRIVING_DESTINATIONS_PLAN_20261108.md), copied from the build ledger in the order made. Each says what it costs if wrong. Results are in [CAMERA_DRIVING.md](CAMERA_DRIVING.md).

- Task 2: Ruling: heuristic = grid cost-to-go over free cells including the guide penalty (8-connected relaxation), not straight-line distance — straight-line took the right turn past 150 ms (2,731 expansions), and weighting it cut the corner into the oncoming lane; now ~30 ms — cost if wrong: plans may be up to ~8% off optimal (grid distance overestimates diagonals).

- Task 3: Ruling: through a junction the path is followed along its length (look-ahead point 4 m along it; obstacle hits tested in 2D against path points) and the road-end check is skipped — a 90-degree turn is not a function of distance ahead, which the existing path_y(x) code assumes; the Hybrid A* plan already avoids what the road model calls not-road — cost if wrong: a road edge the planner widened past would not be braked for separately.
- Task 3: Ruling: the junction goal is the farthest visible route point whose cell is free after widening (plan said "a visible grid cell") — far mask rows are coarse (~1.2 m of ground per row at 20 m), so lane-centre goals fell in the widened road edge and the plan failed — cost if wrong: the goal sits a little nearer than the farthest visible point.
- Task 3: Ruling: a failed re-plan keeps driving the last good plan when nothing blocks it; the route line is used only when there is no plan or it is blocked (plan said: use the guide on no_path/budget) — switching to the tighter route line mid-turn ran the synthetic left turn 0.5 m wide — cost if wrong: a stale plan up to 0.5 s old is followed.
- Task 3: Ruling: after a junction the car steers by the route line until it is within 0.3 m of it and lined up within 5 degrees, then hands back to kerb following (plan: hand back when the kerb fit has 6 rows) — handing back while still turning left the car 0.4 m wide 10 m after the exit — cost if wrong: a few more metres steered by the map line.
- Task 3: Ruling: hold_gap_m = 3.0, not 2.0 — the speed controller runs about 1.2 m past a stopping curve, so aiming at 3 m stops the car ~1.8 m short, as the spec intends ("stops 2 m short") — cost if wrong: it waits about 1 m further back.

- the keep-left offset steps from 1.5 to 1.1 m at 30 m instead of easing.
