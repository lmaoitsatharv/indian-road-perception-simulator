What these scores mean

The percentage is road intersection-over-union: overlap between predicted road and labeled road divided by their combined area. It measures agreement with depth, map geometry and CARLA’s native object labels on these selected synthetic scenes. It is not your reserved real-image test score. The model did not train on these frames, and its predictions did not steer or brake the car.
